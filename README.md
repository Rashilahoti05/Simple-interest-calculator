# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple interest calculator that calculates the simple interest based on the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period in years.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

## Input

- Principal amount
- Annual rate of interest
- Time period in years

## Output

The calculated simple interest.

## Example

If the principal is 1000, the annual interest rate is 5%, and the time period is 2 years:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100
